# Curriculum sources

The student-facing subject and unit lists for these programmes are transcribed
from the following curriculum documents. Theory content areas become MCQ units;
clinical postings, practical activities, and assessment instructions do not.
Subject marks and teaching hours follow the published subject tables. CTEVT
does not assign marks to each theory unit, so unit marks remain zero.

| Programme | Source | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PCL Nursing | [CTEVT revised 2018](https://www.ctevt.org.np/uploads/docs/2019-09-27_PCL%20in%20Nursing%20Revised%202018.pdf) | Years 1–3 |
| Certificate in Medical Laboratory Technology | [CTEVT revised 2024](https://ctevt.org.np/uploads/docs/2024-08-25_CURRICULUM%20for%20CMLT.pdf) | Years 2–3; Year 1 uses the shared health science curriculum |
| Physiotherapy | [CTEVT revised 2025](https://ctevt.org.np/uploads/docs/2025-10-17_PCL%20in%20Medical%20Science%20%28Physiotherapy%29_revision_2025%20%282082%20BS%29.pdf) | Years 2–3; Year 1 uses the shared health science curriculum |
| +2 Science, Computer Science, Management | [NEB curriculum index](https://neb.gov.np/curriculum) and [CDC optional-subject volumes](https://lib.moecdc.gov.np/catalog/opac_css/index.php?id=56&lvl=etagere_see) | Grade 11 and 12 content areas; schools choose elective combinations. |

The current shared CTEVT Year 1, D. Pharmacy, and HA mappings predate this
addition. English, Nepali, and Social Studies are omitted from displayed
curricula. The scanned NPC past-question collection was removed.
