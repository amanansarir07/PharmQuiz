# General Medicine (HA) curriculum mapping

Source: [CTEVT PCL in General Medicine, third revision (2024)](https://www.ctevt.org.np/uploads/docs/2024-08-25_CURRICULUM%20for%20General%20Medicine.pdf), curriculum structure on PDF pages 11–12 and course units on pages 15–105. CTEVT lists this award as **PCL in General Medicine**; HA is the common professional abbreviation.

Year 1 is unchanged in Bujh and uses the existing shared first-year Pharmacy content. This mapping covers only Years 2 and 3. Subjects are grouped by year; theory units are ordered as published. Practical and field placements are included in the source's subject assessment scheme but are not treated as MCQ units. Unit marks are left at zero because CTEVT does not publish a consistent unit-level mark for every subject.

The Year 2 total is **1,175 marks**; Year 3 theory subjects total **350 marks**. The Year 3 comprehensive clinical and community practica are additional placements in the source document.

The source skips the printed label “Unit 7” in Health Education, moving from Unit 6 to Unit 8. The twelve published headings are stored in order with consecutive Bujh unit IDs. Environmental Health has inconsistent unit hours between its headings and marking table; Bujh keeps the hours printed beside the headings and the 105-hour course total. HA question imports published before this curriculum was registered use shorter subject and unit slugs. The reader maps those slugs to the curriculum IDs within each HA year, so existing published MCQs remain available without moving database records. New imports should use the curriculum IDs shown in the admin destination picker.
